Test-Time Geometric Alignment for Camera-Controlled Video Generation

Anonymous authors

This page shows video results for the source-referenced camera-control setting (Track B). A frozen ReCamMaster generator re-renders a source video along a target camera trajectory; the baseline is its unguided single sample (N = 1), and ours selects the best of eight candidates from the same pool with the Pi3 geometric-consistency reward. Each example shows two synchronized comparisons: the RGB outputs, and the per-pixel fused GeCo error rendered over the frames.

How to read these videos.
  • Each panel row is Source | Baseline | Ours, frame-synchronized. All 81 frames are shown, played at the generator’s native 16 fps.
  • In the GeCo videos, warmer colors mean larger fused geometric error (one shared scale per export). Untinted regions are excluded from evaluation (moving subjects, low depth confidence, or pixels without a valid neighboring view) — not zero error. The running number in each panel is that frame’s fused error ×10−3 (lower is better).
  • Headline numbers on each card are full-clip fused GeCo errors ×10−3.
  • These examples are selected high-improvement cases, chosen by ranking per-video GeCo reductions across all 480 matched comparisons; average behavior over the full evaluation is reported in the paper.
